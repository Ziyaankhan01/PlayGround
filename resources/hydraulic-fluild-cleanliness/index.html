Hydraulic Fluid Cleanliness: The 70% Failure Mode Nobody Budgets For

Sep 12, 2026 · 7 min read · Technical Articles

Contamination causes the majority of hydraulic failures. Here is the filtration and monitoring practice NOVARA builds into every HPU.

Start with the fundamentals

Target ISO 4406 18/16/13 or better for proportional systems; verify with periodic bottle sampling.

What the field data shows

Size return filtration at β10 ≥ 200 and protect pump inlets with 100-micron strainers, not fine filters.

The NOVARA practice

Fit desiccant breathers and seal tank lids — airborne ingress defeats even premium filters.

Specification checklist

Log temperature: every 10°C above 60°C halves oil life. Thermostat-controlled cooling pays for itself.

Bottom line

Specify telemetry ports at order stage — retrofitting sensors costs five times more.
