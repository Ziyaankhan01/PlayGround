Company Story

Built in the workshop. Proven in the field.

Founded as a three-machine job shop, NOVARA grew by doing the unglamorous work well: holding tolerances, documenting tests, shipping on time. Today the (fictional) company designs eight product platforms, supports distributors across 40+ countries, and still measures itself by one metric — installed equipment running at nameplate, years later.

  • Engineering-first: application sizing before selling
  • Traceability as standard, not an upsell
  • 20-year spares availability pledge
  • Distributors trained and certified, not just signed
