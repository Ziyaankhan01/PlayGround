NX-Series Industrial Pumps

The NX-Series is NOVARA’s workhorse centrifugal pump platform for continuous-duty process service. A modular wet-end, IE4 drive options and standardised seal chambers cut spare-part inventories while delivering stable duty curves across water, hydrocarbons and mild chemicals.
