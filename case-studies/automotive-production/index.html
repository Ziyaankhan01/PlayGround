Challenge

A body shop adding an EV platform faced 11% unplanned downtime on die-transfer conveyors and a controls architecture that made retooling slow and wiring-heavy.

Solution & Technical Overview

NOVARA replaced three transfer lines with Titan conveyor modules and AX-500 distributed control, reusing existing Siemens PLCs. Servo-positioned turntables and WMS handshakes enabled mixed-model flow.

  • Application-engineered sizing with documented duty assumptions
  • Factory acceptance testing before dispatch
  • Commissioning supervision and operator training included
